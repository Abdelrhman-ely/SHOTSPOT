Map assumptions & limits
  • Drift is a circular, uniform area around the aim point, not a landing prediction.
  • Weakly constrained terrain (purple dots) has no LOLA laser shot within 20 m, so heights are interpolated. It warns you but only affects Pass/Fail when enabled in the mission profile.
  • Surface hazard detection is not part of this prototype. The terrain grid cannot resolve objects smaller than about 10 m.
